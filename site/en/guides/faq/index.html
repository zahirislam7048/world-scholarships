Frequently asked questions

The questions students from Bangladesh ask most about studying abroad — with links to detailed guides and official sources.

Can I study abroad or get a scholarship without IELTS?

In some cases. Many universities accept a Medium of Instruction letter if your previous degree was taught in English, and some accept Duolingo or TOEFL. The Commonwealth Shared Scholarship itself does not require IELTS, and Malaysia's MIS accepts an English-taught degree.

Can I get a scholarship with a low CGPA?

Many scholarships set a minimum (e.g. 3.00 for an MIS master's); many do not state one. Use the eligibility checker and explain weak results briefly in your statement of purpose.

What if I have a study gap?

Rules differ. Some scholarships set limits — DAAD EPOS requires a bachelor's degree no older than 6 years — while others (e.g. the Swedish Institute) require work experience. Explain what you did during the gap.

Are there age limits?

Some scholarships have them: Türkiye Scholarships (master's under 30, PhD under 35), France Eiffel (master's 29, PhD 35), MIS (master's 40, PhD 45). Check the eligibility row on each scholarship page.

How much money must I show for the visa?

It depends on the country — e.g. €11,904 a year in a German blocked account, AUD 29,710 plus course fees for Australia. See Student visas by country.

How can I study without paying tuition?

Three routes: full government scholarships (all scholarships), tuition-free countries such as Germany or Norway, and university funding for a PhD (Funded PhD).

Can I apply without an agent?

Yes. Most universities and government scholarships accept online applications directly, and every scholarship on this site links to the official application. If you use an agent, read Avoiding fraud first.

Can part-time work pay for my studies?

Not fully. Work hours are capped (e.g. 20 hours a week in term in the UK, 1,040 hours a year in Italy), and visa funds must be shown separately.

Do I need to contact a professor before a PhD application?

In many countries yes — especially the UK, Europe, Australia, and for RA funding in the US. Some DAAD scholarships require a supervisor's consent. Use the professor search.

How reliable is this site and how often is it updated?

Everything comes from official websites — governments, embassies, universities or scholarship providers — with a source link on every entry. The list is updated weekly and expired calls are removed. Spotted a mistake? Email higherstudyinfo30@gmail.com.
