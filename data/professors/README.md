# Professor directory

`professors.csv` lists faculty members by university, department and subject, for students looking for an MS/PhD supervisor.

- Source: official university faculty pages only (domain-restricted search). `verification_status=search-snippet`.
- Stored fields: name, title, department, research interests, official profile link and faculty-directory link.
- **No emails or phone numbers are stored.** Students contact professors through the official profile.
- `profile_url` is empty when only the department directory was confirmed; the site then links to the directory.
- Titles change over time. Re-check them during the weekly update and add new universities and subjects over time.

Coverage (2026-11-08): 162 professors, 27 universities, 22 countries. The weekly update adds one university (8–10 professors) per week; Hungary and Taiwan are next.
